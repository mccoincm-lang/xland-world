Xland Forge

Creates Xland's five tokens on Base from your own wallet. Every transaction is signed in your wallet; this page never sees a key.

On Base mainnet these tokens are public and permanent. Nothing is minted here: every supply starts at zero and only roles you grant later can mint. The connected wallet becomes the only admin of all five, so keep its recovery phrase offline. Admin can be handed to a Safe later without recreating anything.
